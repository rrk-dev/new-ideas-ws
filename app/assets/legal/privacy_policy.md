# Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-10-18

This Privacy Policy explains what Lumen LLC ("we", "us") does with information
when you use the Lumen app ("Lumen" or "the app"). It is written to match how
the app actually works. If the app changes in a way that affects this policy,
we update the policy before or with the release.

## Summary in plain language

- **No data is collected by us.** Your recordings, transcripts, notes,
  questions and settings stay on your device. We do not receive, see or
  store them.
- No account, no sign-in, no server that holds your content.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting service is
  built into the app.
- The only network use by the app is downloading AI models you ask for. The
  host that serves a model can see your IP address and which public file you
  asked for, as any web server can. Your content is never in these requests.
- You control your data: you can delete one recording or everything from
  Settings.
- You must be 21 or older to use the app.

## 1. Who is responsible

Lumen LLC is the publisher of the app. Contact for privacy questions:
lumen@lumensupport.org.

Because the app processes your content only on your device and we never
receive it, we do not collect or hold your personal data in the usual sense.
You decide what you record and where it goes. For recordings, transcripts and
notes, you act on your own behalf as the person who controls them.

## 2. What stays on your device

The following is created and kept on your device only:

- **Recordings** (audio you record or import) and **transcripts**.
- **Extracted notes**, summaries, action items, mind maps and the Markdown
  files the app generates.
- **Ask your notes**: your questions, the answers and their citations, if you
  keep chat history (you can turn history off).
- Profiles, templates, vault folder choices and **settings**.
- A record of your acceptance of the Terms and this policy (version and
  time).
- A local **network activity log** (time, host, path, status and size of each
  request the app made; no headers, bodies or content) that you can read and
  clear in Settings.
- Local **crash reports** and a diagnostics export, if you create them. They
  contain technical information and no recording, transcript or note text.
  They leave your device only if you choose to share them.

The app database is encrypted. Audio files and other app files rely on your
operating system's file protection, and the app is set up so that its data is
not included in automatic device backups where the system allows this. If you
back up your device in other ways, or you save exports in a synced folder,
those copies are made by you and your operating system and not by us.

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app has no way to send us:

- your recordings, transcripts, notes, questions or answers;
- your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location;
- advertising identifiers, device identifiers or usage statistics;
- crash or diagnostic data, other than what you choose to share yourself;
- payment details (see "Purchases").

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is very little for us to
give you, correct or delete when you ask. See "Your rights".

## 4. The only network use: model downloads

The app works offline once its models are installed. When you choose to
download a speech or language model, or when it refreshes the signed model
list, it contacts one of these hosts:

- `models.vaultvoice.example` (our model host)
- `huggingface.co` (a public mirror, used for model files)
- `us.aws.cdn.hf.co` (the mirror's file network, which `huggingface.co`
  redirects to)

The request contains the name of a public file and the usual technical
details of a web request. The host receives your IP address and the time, as
any web server would. We do not send cookies, account or device identifiers,
or any of your content. The mirror is a third party, and its own policy applies
to what it logs. Models are checked with a signature and checksum before use.

You can turn on Offline mode in Settings to block all network use by the app
and install models from a file instead. Settings → Privacy → Network activity
shows every request the app has made.

## 5. Purchases

Everything in the app is free at the time of this policy. If we add paid
features, the purchase is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play inside
your operating system. We do not see your payment details. Apple and Google
handle them under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Features that involve other apps or services

- **Folders you choose.** The app writes Markdown notes to folders you pick
  (for example an Obsidian or Logseq vault). If that folder is synced by a
  cloud service, that service receives the files, under your arrangement with
  it.
- **Share and export.** When you use the Share sheet, the receiving app gets
  what you chose to share.
- **Apple Reminders (iOS and macOS).** If you tap to export action items to
  Reminders, the items you selected are written to the Reminders app on your
  device. If you use iCloud or another account to sync Reminders, that is your
  system setting, and the sync is done by your operating system, not by us.
- **Microphone.** The app uses the microphone only after you tap Record, and
  shows when it is recording. It does not record phone or VoIP calls.
- **Notifications** are created on your device by the app, with no push
  service, and show details only if you turn that setting on. **Widgets and
  quick-start shortcuts** contain no recording content and only open the
  app's Record screen.
- **Device diagnostics** you may have turned on in your operating system are
  handled by Apple or Google under their own policies and are not ours.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- You choose how long audio and transcripts or notes are kept in Settings
  (for example keep, delete after export, or delete after a number of days).
- You can delete one recording, one chat or all chat history, or use "Delete
  all data" to remove recordings, transcripts, notes, chats and settings from
  the app.
- Files you exported to other folders or apps are not removed by the app. You
  delete those yourself.
- Uninstalling the app removes its stored data from your device, subject to
  your operating system and any backups you made.
- Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore deleted data.

## 8. Recording other people

You are responsible for recording and consent laws, including consent from
the people you record and the lawful handling of their information. See the
Terms of Use.

## 9. Children

The app is for people aged 21 and over. It is not directed at children, and we
do not knowingly provide it to anyone under 21. We do not collect information
from anyone, including children. If you are under 21, do not use the app.

## 10. Security

Recording, transcription and note extraction run on your device. The app
database is encrypted, the app has an optional lock using your device
biometrics or passcode, and the only connections it opens are the model
downloads above. No system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for
keeping your device and your exported files secure.

## 11. Your rights

Data protection laws may give you rights over personal data, for example to
access, correct, delete, port or object to its use, or to withdraw consent. To
the extent these laws apply to us:

- **India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):** rights to access
  information, correct and erase, to grievance redress, and to nominate
  another person. To the extent we process any personal data about you, we do
  so only as described in this policy.
- **European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR):** rights of access,
  rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the
  right to complain to your data protection authority.
- **California (CCPA / CPRA) and other US states:** rights to know, delete,
  correct and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal
  information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Your recordings and notes are on your device, so you can access, export,
correct and delete them yourself in the app. For anything else, write to
lumen@lumensupport.org. We will reply within the time the law requires. We
may need to tell you that we hold no information about you.
