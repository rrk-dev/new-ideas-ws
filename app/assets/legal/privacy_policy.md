# Privacy policy

> Draft shipped with the app. The final wording is supplied by the business
> and legal side and replaces this file before store submission.

## What VaultVoice does with your data

VaultVoice records audio only when you tap Record, turns it into text on
your device, and saves Markdown notes in the folder you choose.

- **No data is collected.** Recordings, transcripts, notes and settings stay
  on your device. We do not receive them.
- **No tracking, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services** are
  built into the app.
- **No accounts.** You do not sign in.

## The only network use

When you choose to download a speech or language model, the app fetches the
model file from our model host or its mirror. The request contains no
recording content. You can turn on Offline mode to block all network use and
import models from a file instead. Settings shows every request the app made.

## Calls

VaultVoice never records phone or VoIP calls. If a call starts, recording
stops and does not resume on its own.

## Recording other people

Recording laws differ by country and state. Only record people who know they
are being recorded and agree to it.

## Your control

You can delete a recording, a note, or all data from Settings. Deleting all
data removes recordings, transcripts and settings from the device.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your
payment details.
