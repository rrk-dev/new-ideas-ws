VaultVoice beta — please read before testing.

This app does not record phone calls or VoIP calls. It records only after
the tester taps Record, and a visible indicator stays on while the
microphone is open. An incoming call stops capture and the app does not
resume recording when the call ends.

No account is required. On first launch the app downloads speech and
language models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Those files stay on
the device. Audio, transcripts and notes are not uploaded.

The microphone background mode and the Android microphone foreground
service exist only so a recording the tester already started can continue
with the screen off, with the indicator still visible.
