Let me define a term first: spindle means the protein fibres that pull chromosomes apart.
Now let us look at phases of mitosis.
Prophase condenses the chromosomes.
Metaphase lines them up in the middle.
Now let us look at cytokinesis.
The cell membrane pinches in two.
Each daughter cell gets one full set of chromosomes.
