# interview-014 - Interview with Chloe

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Lena: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Lena.
Chloe: Thanks for having me, I am Chloe, and I work as a musician.
Lena: How did you pay for the first year?
Chloe: A small grant covered the equipment. Everything else came from weekend consulting work.
Lena: What are you working on next?
Chloe: A longer field study across three regions. We start collecting data in the spring.
Lena: How did people react when it came out?
Chloe: Mostly with curiosity. A few critics said the method was too slow, and they were partly right.
Lena: What role does the community play?
Chloe: Volunteers found most of our bugs. Without them we would still be guessing.
Lena: Which region will you visit first?
Chloe: Good question, I would rather not answer that today.
Lena: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
