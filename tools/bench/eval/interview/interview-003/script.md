# interview-003 - Interview with Mina

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Marcus: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Marcus.
Mina: Thanks for having me, I am Mina, and I work as an engineer.
Marcus: How did you pay for the first year?
Mina: A small grant covered the equipment —
Marcus: Okay.
Mina: Everything else came from weekend consulting work.
Marcus: What would you tell someone just starting out?
Mina: Write down what you tried, even when it fails. Failed experiments teach you the most.
Marcus: What was your biggest early mistake?
Mina: We trusted the vendor numbers without checking them —
Marcus: Yes, yes.
Mina: Calibration matters more than hardware.
Marcus: How did people react when it came out?
Mina: Mostly with curiosity —
Marcus: I see.
Mina: A few critics said the method was too slow, and they were partly right.
Mina: I will introduce you to my former supervisor by tomorrow.
Marcus: Sounds good, thank you.
Marcus: I will email you the written questions by Friday.
Mina: Sounds good, thank you.
Marcus: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
