# interview-021 - Interview with Mina

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Sofia: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Sofia.
Mina: Thanks for having me, I am Mina, and I work as an engineer.
Sofia: What does a normal day look like?
Mina: I start with the overnight logs, then we review anything that failed. Afternoons are for writing.
Sofia: How big was the team at the beginning?
Mina: There were only three of us in a rented garage, and we shared a single laptop.
Sofia: Which tools do you rely on every day?
Mina: A notebook, a soldering iron and a very old spreadsheet. The spreadsheet has survived four migrations.
Sofia: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
