# interview-013 - Interview with Elena

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Dana: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Dana.
Elena: Thanks for having me, I am Elena, and I work as a founder.
Dana: Tell me about a project that failed.
Elena: We built a mobile version nobody asked for. It cost us six months and taught us to ask first.
Dana: How do you keep a balance?
Elena: I protect my Sundays. No messages, no screens, just a long walk.
Dana: What are you working on next?
Elena: A longer field study across three regions. We start collecting data in the spring.
Dana: What surprised you most?
Elena: People wanted the data far more than the tool itself. I did not expect that at all.
Elena: I will introduce you to my former supervisor.
Dana: Sounds good, thank you.
Dana: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
