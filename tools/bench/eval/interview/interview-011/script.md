# interview-011 - Interview with Ben

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Kwame: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Kwame.
Ben: Thanks for having me, I am Ben, and I work as a pilot.
Kwame: What role does the community play?
Ben: Volunteers found most of our bugs. Without them we would still be guessing.
Kwame: How did the project start?
Ben: It began when we noticed the sensors drifted every winter. Nobody had measured it before.
Kwame: What was your biggest early mistake?
Ben: We trusted the vendor numbers without checking them. Calibration matters more than hardware.
Kwame: Why does this work matter to you?
Ben: Because small errors add up over years. A tiny drift becomes a wrong decision.
Kwame: When will the final report be ready?
Ben: Good question, I would rather not answer that today.
Ben: I will share the photos from the field trip.
Kwame: Sounds good, thank you.
Kwame: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
