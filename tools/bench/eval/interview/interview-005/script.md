# interview-005 - Interview with Gita

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Lena: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Lena.
Gita: Thanks for having me, I am Gita, and I work as a biologist.
Lena: What would you tell someone just starting out?
Gita: Write down what you tried, even when it fails. Failed experiments teach you the most.
Lena: Tell me about a project that failed.
Gita: We built a mobile version nobody asked for. It cost us six months and taught us to ask first.
Lena: How did people react when it came out?
Gita: Mostly with curiosity. A few critics said the method was too slow, and they were partly right.
Lena: How do you keep a balance?
Gita: I protect my Sundays. No messages, no screens, just a long walk.
Gita: I will share the photos from the field trip by Friday.
Lena: Sounds good, thank you.
Gita: I will check the dates in the timeline by the end of the month.
Lena: Sounds good, thank you.
Lena: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
