Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Hugo: I start with the overnight logs, then we review anything that failed. Afternoons are for writing.
Dana: What was the hardest decision you made?
Hugo: Closing the first workshop was painful. We kept the tools and let the lease go.
Hugo: I will check the dates in the timeline.
Dana: Sounds good, thank you.
