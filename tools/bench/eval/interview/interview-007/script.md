# interview-007 - Interview with Ravi

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Tomas: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Tomas.
Ravi: Thanks for having me, I am Ravi, and I work as a researcher.
Tomas: What would you tell someone just starting out?
Ravi: Write down what you tried, even when it fails. Failed experiments teach you the most.
Tomas: How did you pay for the first year?
Ravi: A small grant covered the equipment. Everything else came from weekend consulting work.
Tomas: How did the project start?
Ravi: It began when we noticed the sensors drifted every winter. Nobody had measured it before.
Tomas: How big was the team at the beginning?
Ravi: There were only three of us in a rented garage, and we shared a single laptop.
Tomas: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
