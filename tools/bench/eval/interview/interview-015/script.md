# interview-015 - Interview with Iris

Synthetic two-person interview (fictional people).

Marcus: Welcome, and thank you for joining me. I am Marcus.
Iris: Thanks for having me, I am Iris, and I work as a teacher.
Marcus: How do you keep a balance?
Iris: I protect my Sundays —
Marcus: Okay.
Iris: No messages, no screens, just a long walk.
Marcus: Who influenced you the most?
Iris: My first supervisor —
Marcus: Okay.
Iris: She never gave answers, she only asked better questions.
Marcus: Which tools do you rely on every day?
Iris: A notebook, a soldering iron and a very old spreadsheet —
Marcus: I see.
Iris: The spreadsheet has survived four migrations.
Marcus: What role does the community play?
Iris: Volunteers found most of our bugs. Without them we would still be guessing.
Marcus: I will book a second session by tomorrow.
Iris: Sounds good, thank you.
Marcus: I will email you the written questions by next week.
Iris: Sounds good, thank you.
Marcus: Thank you for your time, that was a great conversation.
