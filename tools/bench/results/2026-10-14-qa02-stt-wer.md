## Interpretation (QA-02, 2026-10-14)
- **Models:** quality ordering small.en-q5_1 (1.5 %) < base.en-q5_1 (2.6 %) < tiny.en (3.7 %);
  base.en-q5_1 is the best quality/size trade-off (57 MB, RTF ≈ 0.04 on 4 CPU threads of an M3
  Pro). Phones are 3–8× slower — device RTF rows in `docs/test-plans/device/QA-02.md` (⏳).
  Residual "numbers" errors are digit-string formatting ("4472" vs "four four seven two"), not
  recognition errors.
- **Software AGC (AUD-02):** no benefit on this corpus — Whisper's log-mel front end already
  normalises level; AGC on is equal (clean), within noise (quiet: +0.15, 1 word) or slightly worse
  in noise (+0.45 / +0.76: AGC also lifts the noise floor between words). Only the quiet+babble
  case improved (−0.61). **Recommendation: keep software AGC off by default** (AUD-02's
  provisional default stands). Platform AGC / noise suppression cannot be emulated on the host:
  decided by the device rows (⏳).
- **VAD trimming (AUD-03):** transcribing only speech (oracle spans + 200 ms padding) cut
  processing time by 35 % (13.9 s → 9.0 s) and removed 2 of 5 insertions in the 8 s pauses
  (WER 2.72 → 2.12 %). The crude energy VAD saved 48 % but lost words at turn edges (2.87 %),
  so trimming quality depends on the VAD's edge padding — AUD-03's Silero VAD should keep
  ≥ 200 ms padding. **Recommendation: trim to speech before STT (AUD-03 default on).**
- n = 3 runs for model timing (median + all runs listed); WER is deterministic (greedy). AGC and
  VAD rows are single runs on the 8 synthetic items (≈ 660 words), so differences below ~0.5
  points (≈ 3 words) are within noise.
