## Gates (task ASK-01)

- Schema validity ≥ 90%: 3B 100.0% (built-in) / 99.4% (all 6); worst single profile 96.7% (lecture, 1 of 30 cases) → ✅
- Hallucination ≤ 15%: 3B 5.5% (built-in) / 4.4% (all 6); worst single profile 13.6% (warranty) → ✅
- Peak RSS budgets (`tools/bench/llm_select/candidates.json`): Low 1500 MiB, Mid 2800 MiB, High 4200 MiB. 3B worst case 3718 MiB → fits **High only**; fails Mid and Low.
- Model load + Dart startup (1-case run, upper bound): 1B ≈ 5 s, 3B ≈ 11 s (wall 12 s / 32 s minus the 7.2 s / 21.0 s case latency).

## Reading

- Quality is **mixed per profile, not uniformly better**: the 3B is far better on warranty (92% vs 45%; hallucination 13.6% vs 34.4%) and better on meeting and lecture, but clearly worse on engineering (52% vs 100%; trade-off recall 9.4% against its 70% target) and interview (82% vs 91%). Averaged it is about equal (80% vs 78% built-in), hallucinates less (5.5% vs 13.4%), decodes ~2× slower on the same CPU (≈ 21–33 tok/s vs ≈ 46–70) and peaks at ~1.9× the memory.
- Decision: **recommended for the High tier only** (`recommended_device_tiers: ["high"]`, `min_ram_mb: 7168`). **Not recommended for Mid (5–7 GiB) or Low (< 5 GiB)**: the measured peak (3.6 GiB) exceeds the Mid budget (2.8 GiB). Mid stays "not recommended" until the device rows in `docs/test-plans/device/ASK-01.md` pass. Because the quality gain is profile-dependent, ASK-03 (ask-your-notes) should evaluate the 3B on its own question-answering eval, and the engineering/interview regressions are a prompt follow-up before the 3B becomes an automatic default (see Follow-ups in the task file).
- Host numbers ≠ device numbers: device tok/s and footprint are ⏳ in the device checklist.
